# Chapter 1 — Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Artificial Intelligence is no longer limited to the digital world.  
The next major leap in technology is **Physical AI**—intelligent systems that can sense, think, and act in the real, physical environment.

This book explores the world of **Embodied Intelligence**, where AI interacts with gravity, friction, balance, objects, humans, and unpredictable environments. Humanoid robots are the perfect platform for this evolution because they share our shape, tools, and spaces.

---

## 🌍 What is Physical AI?

**Physical AI** refers to AI systems that operate in the real world, not only on screens.  
They must understand:

- Physics  
- Motion  
- Objects  
- Space  
- Human behavior  
- Sensor inputs  
- Real-time decision making  

Unlike a ChatGPT agent that only predicts text, a physical robot must:

1. Perceive (Camera, LiDAR, IMU)
2. Think (LLMs, planners, navigation)
3. Act (motors, joints, controllers)

This creates a **Digital Brain → Physical Body** connection.

---

## 🤖 Why Humanoid Robots?

Humanoid robots are rising for one simple reason:

### **The world is built for humans.**

Door handles, stairs, chairs, kitchens, warehouses — everything is designed for a human-shaped body.

Humanoids excel because:

- They fit into existing human environments  
- They can use human tools  
- They can be trained with massive real-world data  
- They can help in homes, factories, hospitals  

Companies like **Tesla (Optimus)**, **Figure AI**, and **Unitree** are already racing ahead.
